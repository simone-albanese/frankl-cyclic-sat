# Z15 — RESOLVED: UNSAT with a verified certificate (14 Aug 2026)

**Theorem.** No nontrivial union-closed family $F \subseteq \mathcal{P}([15])$
invariant under rotation ($\mathbb{Z}_{15}$), with nontrivial member sets of
size ≥ 3 (Sarvate–Renaud reduction), violates Frankl's conjecture: the integer
margin $2\cdot\mathrm{maxfreq} - |F|$ is always ≥ 0.

This closes **open problem 1** of this repository, published 12 Aug 2026 when
the original run was stopped: verifying the projected DRAT certificate was
estimated at 11–18 GB of RAM against 16 GB physical. The wall was the proof
*format* — DRAT checkers hold the whole proof in memory; **LRAT** checkers
verify in a single forward stream with memory proportional to the *formula*.

## Evidence chain (exceeds the project standard)

1. **Method 1 — CP-SAT (OR-Tools 9.15.6755)**, native model (margin as an
   integer linear constraint over 2,190 orbit variables): INFEASIBLE in 889 s
   (T9a, 11 Aug 2026; `results/logs/T9a_z15_cpsat_min3.log`).
2. **Method 2 — CaDiCaL 3.0.1** on the independent frozen CNF
   (`z15min3.cnf`, binary-adder margin encoding, 16,856 vars / 28,850,111
   clauses, sha256
   `e6c732cf30bc619dd4c2706734bdcc2ed99255a422c52c4a8525563785115120` —
   regenerated byte-identical before the run): **exit 20 = UNSAT** in
   **73,544.58 s (20 h 26 m)**, max RSS 4,793.61 MB, finished
   2026-08-14 18:57:39 CEST. Launched unbounded 13 Aug 22:31:17 CEST.
3. **Certificate** — text LRAT proof, **158,233,546,333 bytes (147 GB)**,
   sha256
   `9d8b6b722236cd45da5a604494d0f12072e30a163268aab58ee14d6e83ec0dd3`.
   Verified by `lrat-check` (drat-trim repository): **"c VERIFIED"** in
   **1,358.68 s (22.6 min)** — 145,241,055 added / 130,164,827 deleted
   clauses, max live clauses 28,850,111 (= the formula), i.e. streaming
   verification RAM tracks the formula, not the 147 GB proof.

The two methods share only the orbit/closure generator (validated on the
Z7/Z11/P([4]) controls); margin encoding, search engines and codebases are
independent.
